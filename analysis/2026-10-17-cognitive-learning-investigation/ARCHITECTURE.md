## Additional integration observations

**Fast learning can be overwritten by slow reconstruction.** Goal success can remove a weak-area label immediately, while symbolic self-model rebuilding derives labels again from rule/domain statistics. The revision ledger intended to preserve the correction is not read by that rebuild. This is a concrete instance of stabilization logic overriding revision; whether the alternative domain-quality route compensates depends on actual state.

**Activity changes several learners at once.** One cognitive call can alter memory, a reward EMA, a bandit bucket, a signal association, a causal edge and later a chain bonus. Agreement among those records is not independent corroboration when all derive from the same proxy event.

**Costs are partly real; benefits are often proxies.** Latency and resource telemetry can be directly measured. Reward and progress often depend on classification, counts, mentions or structural novelty. This asymmetry can make an apparently coherent controller optimize inexpensive internally meaningful activity rather than difficult externally useful work.

**The symbol/neural boundary is asymmetric.** Symbolic state supplies language-conditioning material and filters neural output, while language training mostly improves emission. The grounded subprocess learner supplies an unusually clear supervised feedback loop, but is not the language organ and is not the principal autonomous action learner. Calling all three “learning” hides materially different capabilities.

**Source-level isolation is not perfect.** For example, `native_lm.py` constructs its checkpoint directory from `__file__`, unlike acquisition's `DATA_DIR`-resolved corpus. That is a potential state-isolation defect, not a demonstrated cause of the behavioral ruts. It reinforces why this investigation used no runtime imports and why future experiments must verify isolation before executing training paths.

The architecture is richly connected, with several useful negative-feedback controls. The central unresolved question is not whether outputs have *any* consumer, but whether valid new evidence has enough preserved, correctly attributed authority to change the decision that matters.
