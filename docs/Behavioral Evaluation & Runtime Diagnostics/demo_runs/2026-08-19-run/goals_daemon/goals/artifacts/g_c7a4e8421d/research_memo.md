In biology , homology is similarity in anatomical structures or genes between organisms of different taxa due to shared ancestry , regardless of current functional differences. Evolutionary biology explains homologous structures as retained heredity from a common ancestor after having been subjected to adaptive modifications for different purposes as the result of natural selection .
