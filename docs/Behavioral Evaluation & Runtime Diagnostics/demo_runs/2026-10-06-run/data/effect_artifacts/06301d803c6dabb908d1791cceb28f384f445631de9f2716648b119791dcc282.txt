Not to be confused with Ethics or Asceticism . Aesthetics [ a ] is the branch of philosophy that studies beauty , taste , and related phenomena. In a broad sense, it includes the philosophy of art , which examines the nature of art , artistic creativity , the meanings of artworks, and audience appreciation. Aesthetic properties are features that influence the appeal of objects. They include aesthetic values, which express positive or negative qualities, such as the contrast between beauty and ugliness . Philosophers debate whether aesthetic properties have objective existence or depend on the subjective experiences [ b ] of observers. According to a common view, aesthetic experiences are associated with disinterested pleasure detached from practical concerns.
