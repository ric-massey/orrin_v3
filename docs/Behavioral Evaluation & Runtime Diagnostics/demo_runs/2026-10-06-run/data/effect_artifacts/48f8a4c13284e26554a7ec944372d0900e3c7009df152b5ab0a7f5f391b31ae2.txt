Charles Darwin noted that having similar embryos implied common ancestry, but little progress was made until the 1970s. Then, recombinant DNA technology at last brought embryology together with molecular genetics . Contingency was especially emphasized by Stephen Jay Gould , particularly in his 1989 book Wonderful Life .
