On television, she is known for her roles in the BBC One series Howards End (2017), the ITV series Harlots (2017–2018), the Apple TV+ series Slow Horses (2022–2026) and the Netflix series Missing You (2025). Her films include The Personal History of David Copperfield (2019). Early life and education [ edit ] Eleazar was born to a British mother and a Ghanaian father. [ 1 ] As a child, she “intermittently” saw her father Courage Quashigah , a politician who served as Ghana's Minister of State for Agriculture and later Health between 2001 and 2009. [ 2 ] She was primarily raised by her mother in Clapham , London. [ 3 ] Eleazar earned an academic scholarship to attend Gordonstoun School in Moray , Scotland. [ 1 ] She studied Spanish and Mandarin at Nottingham University before moving to Ghana to work in her brother’s production company. [ 3 ] [ 1 ] In her mid-20s, she moved back to the United Kingdom and trained at the London Academy of Music and Dramatic Art from where she graduated in 2015. [ 4 ] [ 5 ] Career [ edit ] Eleazar appeared in Plaques and Tangles at the Royal Court Theatre in 2015. [ 6 ] She made her television debut in 2016 with appearances in an episode of the BBC One medical soap opera Holby City , the Channel 4 miniseries National Treasure as Georgina and the BBC Two adaptation of Zadie Smith 's NW as Shar. The following year, she had her first main roles in the BBC One miniseries Rellik as Christine Levison [ 7 ] and Howards End as Jacky Bast, [ 8 ] and joined the recurring cast of the ITV Encore and Hulu period drama Harlots as Violet Cross. [ 4 ] In 2019, Eleazar made her feature film debut as Agnes in Armando Iannucci 's The Personal History of David Copperfield [ 9 ] and played Kate in the ITV series Deep Water . [ 10 ] On the West End stage, Eleazar appeared in The Starry Messenger [ 11 ] at Wyndham's Theatre and as Yelena in Uncle Vanya at the Harold Pinter Theatre . [ 12 ] In 2022, Eleazar began starring as Louisa Guy in the Apple TV+ spy thriller Slow Horses . She had a main role as Dr. Vivienne McMahon in the 2023 FX on Hulu miniseries Class of '09 . She returned to the stage in The House of Bernarda Alba at the National Theatre . In 2025, Eleazar led Harlan Coben 's Netflix miniseries Missing You as Kat Donovan. [ 13 ] She also appeared in the film Rose of Nevada . Personal life [ edit ] Eleazer married Italian actor and producer Gabriele Lo Giudice in September 2023. [ 14 ] The couple live in east London. [ 3 ] Eleazer has symbrachydactyly , a congenital abnormality characterized by short, missing or webbed fingers.
