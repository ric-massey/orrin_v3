Sociobiology is a field of biology that aims to explain social behavior in terms of evolution . It draws from disciplines including ethology and evolutionary biology. Within the study of human societies , sociobiology is closely related to evolutionary anthropology and human behavioral ecology . Sociobiology investigates social behaviors such as the hive society of social insects . It argues that just as selection pressure led to animals evolving useful ways of interacting with the natural environment , so it led to the genetic evolution of advantageous social behavior. While the term 'sociobiology' originated at least as early as the 1940s, the concept did not gain major recognition until the publication of E. O.
