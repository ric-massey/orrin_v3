History [ edit ] Eight of its eleven songs are studio re-recordings of songs from his first (live) album 12-String Blues . Although they should have been improved by being recorded in a professional studio, there is noticeable oversaturation in many of the songs, causing his vocal to break up. Kottke has often been quoted as being unhappy with the recording quality. [ 1 ] It has not been re-issued on CD.
