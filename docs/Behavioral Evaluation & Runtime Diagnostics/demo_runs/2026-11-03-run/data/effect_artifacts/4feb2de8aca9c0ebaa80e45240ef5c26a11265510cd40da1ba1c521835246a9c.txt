Assassination attempts on Hirohito - Wikipedia Jump to content From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia Hirohito circa 1935. During the 1920s and '30s, there were three known assassination attempts on Hirohito , the Emperor of Japan . [ 1 ] The assailants were all either Korean or Japanese . Assassination attempts on Hirohito took place throughout his reign as prince regent , and Emperor of Japan . All of their attempts failed. All four would-be assassins were sentenced to death, though one was granted amnesty and eventually released, and one committed suicide in prison. [ citation needed ] In 1923, Daisuke Namba attempted to assassinate Hirohito. [ 2 ] Fumiko Kaneko and Pak Yeol both plotted to assassinate the emperor in 1925. [ 3 ] Lee Bong-chang attempted to assassinate the Emperor in 1932, [ 4 ] [ 5 ] in what became known as the Sakuradamon incident .
