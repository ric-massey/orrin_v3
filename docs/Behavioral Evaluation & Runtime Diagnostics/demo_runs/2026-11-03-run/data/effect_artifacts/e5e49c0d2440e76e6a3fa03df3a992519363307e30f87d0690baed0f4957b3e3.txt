Watch Video Center for Gynepathology Research Publication Date : November 3, 2026 Press Inquiries Press Contact : Cassie Gooding Email: cgood@mit.edu MIT Center for Gynopathology Research Close Endometriosis affects more than 265 million women worldwide — 1 out of every 10 women of reproductive age, according to the United Nations — yet it remains one of the most misunderstood, under-researched, and chronically stigmatized conditions in modern medicine. A new documentary, “End of the Cycle,” presented by The Endometriosis Collective, is changing that conversation about this painful disease, one powerful story at a time. Directed by Sammy Jaye and Soraya Simi, the film weaves together intimate personal narratives, cutting-edge science, historical revelations, and cultural commentary to challenge decades of misconceptions. The result is both deeply personal and urgently scientific, sending out a clarion call for research investment, medical accountability, and systemic change. The film features a talented ensemble of women living with endometriosis — comedian Amy Schumer, U.S. Olympian Brittany Brown, actress Janel Parrish, actress and musician Fọlákẹ́ Olówòfóyèkù, dancer Julianne Hough, and influencer and filmmaker Sammy Jaye Lang. When asked to describe endometriosis in three words, their responses cut to the heart of the condition: “agony, isolation, harmful.” The film also profiles 20th century icon Marilyn Monroe, who struggled with endometriosis until her death in 1962 at age 36.
