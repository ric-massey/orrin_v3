[ 1 ] Evolutionary developmental biology , informally known as evo-devo , is a field of biological research that compares the developmental processes of different organisms to infer how developmental processes evolved . The field grew from 19th-century beginnings, where embryology faced a mystery: zoologists did not know how embryonic development was controlled at the molecular level. Charles Darwin noted that having similar embryos implied common ancestry, but little progress was made until the 1970s. Then, recombinant DNA technology at last brought embryology together with molecular genetics .
