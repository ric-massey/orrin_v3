Elizabeth Morris, Matt Mitchell, and Vicki Sargent wrote the screenplay from a story by Owen. Elba also stars in the film alongside Caitlin FitzGerald and Hero Fiennes Tiffin in a story about three astronauts trapped in their capsule as it sinks in the ocean. Premise [ edit ] Three astronauts are returning to Earth when their vessel is forced off course during re-entry. They struggle to survive after being cut off from contact as their capsule sinks into the ocean. Cast [ edit ] Idris Elba as Jackson Caitlin FitzGerald as Brooks Hero Fiennes Tiffin as Rhodes Production [ edit ] Above the Below was announced by Lionsgate at the 2023 American Film Market with Idris Elba and Martin Owen co-directing the film. Owen created the story for the film, with the script being written by Elizabeth Morris, Matt Mitchell, and Vicki Sargent. [ 1 ] The following month it was announced that Elba would also star in the film with Caitlin FitzGerald and Hero Fiennes Tiffin . [ 2 ] Filming began in November 2023 in London , taking place at Pinewood Studios to utilize the underwater studio there, [ 2 ] and it was completed by February 2024. [ 3 ] [ 4 ] Roger Goula was hired to compose the score. [ 5 ] Release [ edit ] The film is scheduled to be released on 4 December 2026.
