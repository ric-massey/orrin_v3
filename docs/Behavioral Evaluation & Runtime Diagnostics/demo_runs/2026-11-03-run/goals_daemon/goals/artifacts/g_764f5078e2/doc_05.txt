While attending Our Lady of Mercy High School , a Catholic high school, Smith developed an eating disorder , lost her faith in God and realized that she was a lesbian , all after her brother's death. The book has received significant acclaim. It was named one of People Magazine ’s ten best books of 2004 and won the Barnes Noble Discover Award for Non-Fiction, the 2005 Lambda Literary Award for Autobiography/Memoir and the 2005 Judy Grahn Award .
