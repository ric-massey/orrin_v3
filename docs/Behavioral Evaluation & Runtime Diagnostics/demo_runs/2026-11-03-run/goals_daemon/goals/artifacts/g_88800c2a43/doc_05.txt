List of Ghosts (American TV series) episodes - Wikipedia Jump to content From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia Ghosts is an American television sitcom about married New Yorkers Samantha and Jay Arondekar, who believe that their dreams have come true when they inherit a beautiful country house, only to find that it is falling apart and inhabited by ghosts who died on the property and are now bound to the area, appearing as they did at the times of their deaths, until they can reach the afterlife.It was adapted to American television for CBS by Joe Port and Joe Wiseman from the British series of the same name . Port Wiseman serve as showrunners. [ 1 ] The American series premiered on October 7, 2021. [ 2 ] In February 2025, the series was renewed for a fifth and sixth season. [ 3 ] The sixth season premiered on October 29, 2026. [ 4 ] As of October 29, 2026 , [ update ] 96 episodes of Ghosts have aired, currently in its sixth season.
