Fox Entertainment Studios Original release Network Fox Release January 4, 2026 ( 2026-01-04 ) – present Best Medicine is an American medical comedy drama television series created by Dominic Minghella . The show is based on the popular British television series Doc Martin . That series' lead actor, Martin Clunes appeared as a guest actor. The show premiered on Fox on January 4, 2026. In March 2026, Fox renewed the series for a second season, which premiered on September 20, 2026. Premise [ edit ] Brilliant but brusque Dr. Martin Best moves from a prestigious hospital position in Boston to a private practice in the small fishing village of Port Wenn, Maine , where his aunt lives and where he summered as a child. As he solves mysteries behind unusual health conditions affecting the townsfolk, he struggles to integrate with them as well as dealing with his past: he walked away from being a surgeon in Boston after developing a pathological blood phobia , resulting from an operation on a young girl who reminded him of his older sister, Rosemary, who died in a car crash as a child.
