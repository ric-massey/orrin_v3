He created and hosted the half-hour truTV show Adam Ruins Everything , based on the CollegeHumor series of the same name. [ 2 ] He was also the host of the American version of The Crystal Maze on Nickelodeon. In 2022, Conover's limited series The G Word with Adam Conover debuted on Netflix . [ 3 ] Conover serves on the board of the Writers Guild of America West . [ 4 ] He also hosts the podcast Factually! With Adam Conover . [ 5 ] He has voiced "A Ryan Secrest Type" as well as other characters on BoJack Horseman . Early life and education [ edit ] Members of the sketch comedy group Olde English . From left to right: Ben Popik , Raphael Bob-Waksberg , David Segal, Caleb Bark, and Adam Conover Adam Conover was born in Smithtown , New York, [ 6 ] and grew up in nearby Wading River , on the North Shore of Long Island . [ 7 ] His parents are David O. Conover , a marine biologist, [ 8 ] and Margaret Conover (née Howard), a botanist . [ 6 ] [ 7 ] [ 8 ] His younger sister, Emily , is a science reporter who holds a Ph.D. in particle physics . [ 9 ] Conover has described himself as "the only member of the family without a Ph.D." [ 10 ] As a teen, he designed webpages and taught himself HTML . [ 11 ] In 2000, he graduated from Shoreham-Wading River High School , where he became interested in performing arts. As a teenager, he was part of a music band called Bocoach. [ 12 ] He then attended Bard College , where, in 2002, he became an active member of the sketch comedy group Olde English . [ 13 ] In 2004, [ 14 ] he earned a bachelor's degree in philosophy from Bard College. [ 7 ] Career [ edit ] In 2012, Conover began working as a sketch comedy writer and performer for the website and YouTube channel CollegeHumor , where he eventually created the web series that would go on to become Adam Ruins Everything on TruTV . [ 7 ] The first episode of the TruTV show, which aims to reveal untold truths and to dispel common misconceptions , [ 15 ] premiered in September 2015. Conover has said his character in the show is a comedic interpretation of the person he worries he once was or is. [ 16 ] As he told The New York Observer , "It's the reaction I've gotten my whole life: that I learn something and try to tell people in conversation, but when I tell them, they are annoyed." [ 16 ] Conover at the 2018 CSICon In 2018, Conover was the headliner of the first night of the annual CSICon conference for skeptics . [ 17 ] He has voiced several characters in the Netflix animated series BoJack Horseman , including A Ryan Seacrest Type , one of the Paparazzi Birds , and Bradley Hitler-Smith . [ 7 ] [ 18 ] In 2019, he voiced the character Big_Hairy_Stallion_69 on the Netflix series Tuca Bertie , and he created the Earwolf podcast Factually! [ 19 ] [ 20 ] In 2020, Conover hosted the American adaptation of the British family game show The Crystal Maze for Nickelodeon . [ 21 ] In November, he made a cameo appearance as his character from Adam Ruins Everything in The Eric Andre Show . [ 22 ] [ 23 ] In December, he announced that Adam Ruins Everything was no longer in production and that he was planning a new Netflix series about the federal government, called The G Word with Adam Conover . The show premiered in May 2022 and ran for six episodes. [ 24 ] Conover at a picket line during the 2023 Writers Guild of America strike Conover serves on the board of the Writers Guild of America West , [ 4 ] and he sat on the 2023 WGA contract negotiating committee. [ 25 ] In 2025, Conover released a video promoting the cryptocurrency project World Network . [ 26 ] [ 27 ] He subsequently released an apology video, said he would not accept any further money from World, and called World's Orb "the creepiest tech product Silicon Valley has ever produced". [ 28 ] Personal life [ edit ] Conover was once a roommate of BoJack Horseman creator Raphael Bob-Waksberg in New York City, who introduced him to his former partner, illustrator Lisa Hanawalt . [ 29 ] Conover had a 15-year relationship with Hanawalt. [ 30 ] [ 31 ] Conover quit drinking in early 2018. [ 32 ] However, according to his 2026 stand-up special Adam Conover: Big Divorce Energy , he has since taken it up again. [ 33 ] In the same special, he said he had joined the dating app Feeld and that he marked his sexual orientation as heteroflexible . [ 33 ] Political views [ edit ] In 2020, Conover was an outspoken supporter of Nithya Raman , who challenged and beat David Ryu for the Los Angeles City Council . As an activist for Housing First policies, Conover performed stand-up to fundraise for the campaign and canvassed in support of Raman. [ 34 ] Filmography [ edit ] This section needs more citations .
