List of scientific debates - Wikipedia Jump to content From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia This is a list of notable scientific debates in the history of science and mathematics . Biology [ edit ] Nature versus nurture , long-standing debate in biology on human behavior 1764–1778 Voltaire vs Needham, between Voltaire and John Needham and others on the validity of preformationism . [ 1 ] 1830 Cuvier–Geoffroy debate between Georges Cuvier and Étienne Geoffroy Saint-Hilaire on animal structure. [ 2 ] 1860 Oxford evolution debate between several scientists on Charles Darwin 's On the Origin of Species . [ 3 ] 1860–1862 Great Hippocampus Question , between Thomas Henry Huxley and Richard Owen on the importance of hippocampus minor . [ 4 ] Chemistry and nanoscience [ edit ] 2001–2003 Drexler–Smalley debate on molecular nanotechnology by K. Eric Drexler and Richard Smalley on the feasibility of molecular assemblers . [ 5 ] Mathematics [ edit ] 1654–1665 problem of points , correspondence between Blaise Pascal and Pierre de Fermat on games and the fair share of a prize pot. [ 6 ] 1655–1679 Hobbes–Wallis controversy , between Thomas Hobbes and John Wallis on geometry and other topics. [ 7 ] 1745 Leibniz–Bernoulli correspondence, between Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz and Johann Bernoulli on the logarithm of negative numbers. [ 8 ] 1902–1924 Nekrasov–Markov conflict, between Pavel Nekrasov and Andrey Markov on the relation between the law of large numbers and free will . [ 9 ] 1903–1912 Poincaré vs. Russell, between Henri Poincaré and Bertrand Russell on logicism and mathematical paradoxes. [ 10 ] 1907–1928 Brouwer–Hilbert foundational debate or Grundlagenstreit , between L. E. J. Brouwer and David Hilbert on foundations of mathematics. [ 11 ] Physics and astronomy [ edit ] 1715–1716 Leibniz–Clarke correspondence , between Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz and Samuel Clarke on space being absolute. [ 12 ] 1857–1901 Kelvin–Stokes aether drag debate , on whether the luminiferous aether was unstable when considered a perfect fluid . Debate between Lord Kelvin and George Stokes . [ 13 ] [ 14 ] 1895 Lübeck Debate between Ludwig Boltzmann and Wilhelm Ostwald on the existence of atoms . [ 15 ] 1920 Great Debate (astronomy) between Harlow Shapley and Heber Curtis on the scale of spiral nebulae and the Universe. [ 16 ] 1920 Bad Nauheim Debate , between Philipp Lenard and Albert Einstein on the validity of general relativity . [ 17 ] See also Criticism of the theory of relativity . 1922 Einstein–Bergson debate between Albert Einstein and philosopher Henri Bergson , on the implications of theory of relativity and the nature of time. [ 18 ] 1927–1935 Bohr–Einstein debates between Albert Einstein and Niels Bohr on interpretations of quantum mechanics . [ 19 ] 1936–1944 Chandrasekhar–Eddington dispute , between Subrahmanyan Chandrasekhar and Arthur Eddington on the limits of star masses. [ 20 ] 1949–1964 Big Bang vs. steady-state , dispute between Fred Hoyle supporter of the steady-state model of the universe and George Gamow supporter of the Big Bang . [ 21 ] Philosophy of science [ edit ] 1965 Kuhn–Popper debate between philosophers Thomas Kuhn and Karl Popper on scientific research methodology. [ 22 ] 1990s Science wars , series of debates on the role of natural sciences over social sciences Science education [ edit ] 1989 Math wars , series of debates on the mathematics education curriculum in the United States Theology and science [ edit ] 2014 Bill Nye–Ken Ham debate by Bill Nye and Ken Ham on science and creationism and evolution .
