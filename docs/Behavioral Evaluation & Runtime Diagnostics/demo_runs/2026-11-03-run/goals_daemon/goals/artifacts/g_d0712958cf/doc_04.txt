The band members who will be performing with Phoebe Bridgers consists of: Yi-Mei Templeman playing cello, Micah Preite playing guitar, Lily Belknap Honigberg playing fiddle, Nick White playing keys, and Connor Vance playing mandolin and violin. [ 1 ] The tour began on September 14 in Indianapolis , Indiana and will conclude on December 12 in Stockholm , Sweden . [ 2 ] [ 3 ] The tour will feature Alex G , Isaac Wood , and Anaïs as the opening acts. [ 4 ] [ 5 ] The tour's United States and United Kingdom shows sold out by June 2026. [ 6 ] The tour will be completely phone free, utilizing Yondr pouches. [ 7 ] [ 8 ] Setlist [ edit ] This setlist is representative of the show on October 28, 2026, in Salt Lake City . It does not represent all dates throughout the tour.
