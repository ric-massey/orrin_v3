Terminism (logic) - Wikipedia Jump to content From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia Terminism in logic refers to a historical development in medieval scholastic logic . [ 1 ] Terminism is defined by rhetorician Walter J. Ong , who links it to nominalism , as "a concomitant of the highly quantified formal logic of medieval scholastic philosophy , and thus contrasts with theology which had closer connections with metaphysics and special commitments to rhetoric" (135).
