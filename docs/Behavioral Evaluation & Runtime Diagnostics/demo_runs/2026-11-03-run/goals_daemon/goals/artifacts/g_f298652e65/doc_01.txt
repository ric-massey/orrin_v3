It is due to be the eighth main entry in the Grand Theft Auto series , following 2013's Grand Theft Auto V , and the sixteenth entry overall. Set within the fictional US state of Leonida, based on Florida , the story follows the criminal duo of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Players will be able to freely roam Leonida's open world , which features the Miami -inspired Vice City . Early development began after Red Dead Redemption 2 ' s release in 2018, following years of preliminary work, and principal production began in 2020. Rockstar's global development teams more than doubled since Red Dead Redemption 2 . Miami and its surrounding areas were extensively researched for the game's world, which parodies 2020s American culture . The protagonists' dynamic is inspired by Bonnie and Clyde , and the non-player characters (NPCs) were written to ensure players experience different areas of Vice City. Following years of speculation and anticipation, Rockstar confirmed in February 2022 that the game was in development. That September, footage from unfinished versions was leaked in what journalists described as one of the most prominent leaks in the history of the video game industry . Grand Theft Auto VI was formally revealed in December 2023. Following two delays, further leaks, and an extensive marketing campaign, it is scheduled to be released on 19 November 2026 for the PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S . Players can choose to fight non-player characters (NPCs), steal vehicles, rob establishments or get into high-speed chases with police officers. [ 8 ] The game will have a "Criminal Profile" rating system that tracks each player character 's reputation based on their actions, similar to the honour system previously used in Red Dead Redemption 2 (2018). [ 9 ] Rockstar has stated this system is not meant to portray Lucia and Jason as "do-gooders", but will track whether each character keeps their crimes professional or commits unnecessary violence with in-game consequences that reflect their reputation. [ 10 ] In free roam, as Jason or Lucia, players can choose to split the two up to go and do their own activities independently or stay, hang out and do activities together. [ 11 ] Their relationship will depend on the player's actions [ 12 ] and, according to Rockstar, will be optional. [ 13 ] Grand Theft Auto VI will feature a fully integrated social media app called Snapmatic, returning from Grand Theft Auto V . [ 16 ] Players will have the option to customise the appearance of Jason and Lucia, such as changing their hair, clothes and accessories. [ 45 ] Research and design Miami and its surrounding areas were extensively researched for the game. The passage of time after the release of Grand Theft Auto V , according to Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson, meant that there was immense pressure for developers to produce a sequel which surpassed fan expectations. [ 16 ] They conceived Grand Theft Auto VI ' s creative direction by examining older games for ideas they believed were incomplete due to time constraints or technological limitations. [ 16 ] One such idea was a story placing romance as a central focus of the gameplay. [ 16 ] The work for the characters concentrated on immersion and how they, and in turn the players, would interact with the game world, especially in free roam. [ 16 ] This resulted in the creation of mechanics, for example, that generate changes in the appearance of characters based on circumstance, such as eating, exercising, and evading police. [ 16 ] Rockstar undertook extensive research of Miami and its surrounding areas with a field team. [ 16 ] Starting in 2015, they organised research trips to Miami to consult with locals to gather reference material, such as law enforcement, club promoters , ex-criminals, and gun dealers. [ 16 ] [ 31 ] The material was then cross-referenced with books and new articles to "get to the heart of which characters will bring [Vice City] to life." [ 16 ] Writer Michael Wiafe said the studio's vision for Vice City was one which replicated the culture of Miami with "an open mind, a genuine appreciation and a deep curiosity". [ 16 ] Rockstar deployed two subteams for the creation of NPCs and vehicles, the former from their Los Angeles office. The developers greatly expanded the population of NPCs, varying each character by appearance, movement, and expression. [ 16 ] More than 50 street artists were engaged to design murals for Vice City neighborhoods. [ 16 ] Rockstar worked with Miami-based stylist Jillian Carr from 2023 to design clothing mimicking real-life fashion trends. [ 16 ] Gameplay Grand Theft Auto VI puts increased focus on realism and immersion in its gameplay systems compared to previous Grand Theft Auto titles, incorporating gameplay elements originally developed for Red Dead Redemption 2 . Rockstar sought increased realism where it can help player immersion while feeling that some forms of realism in gameplay could become inconvenient or tedious for the player. [ 46 ] At points during gameplay systems development, Rockstar had to pull back on some realistic role-playing elements such as the player needing to buy groceries and restock the fridge in Jason and Lucia's safehouse. Rockstar said the experiment was not implemented into the game as it would have "complicated the mechanics" as they sought to strike a balance between realism and ease of play. [ 47 ] Rockstar implemented a limited weapons loadout system previously designed for Red Dead Redemption 2 into Grand Theft Auto VI for added realism and to incentivise player decision-making on choosing loadout options. Rather than the unlimited weapon inventory system from previous series entries, Grand Theft Auto VI restricts the player to carrying two primary firearms and two sidearms at once. [ 48 ] Weapons are stored and transported in the player's vehicle, much like weapons being stored on the player's horse in Red Dead Redemption 2 . [ 49 ] However, building the loadout system into a Grand Theft Auto title reportedly took years of development time as Rockstar struggled to decide on how to they should approach its implementation. [ 50 ] Rockstar were aware that players would notice missing gaps in the weapon UI due to the limited loadout so sought to redesign the UI accordingly. They struggled with formulating a new weapons UI as they kept prototyping alternatives before ending up back at a version of the weapon wheel. [ 50 ] Narrative and writing Grand Theft Auto VI is the first mainline Grand Theft Auto story written without the involvement of Dan Houser since Grand Theft Auto (1997), [ 51 ] [ 52 ] as Houser left Rockstar in 2020. [ 53 ] The initial outline for Grand Theft Auto VI ' s narrative had taken shape by late 2019 when it was decided it would feature the criminal couple of Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in a Bonnie and Clyde inspired story. [ 31 ] Grand Theft Auto VI takes inspiration from Red Dead Redemption 2 in its story being structured around distinct chapters. [ 54 ] Rockstar wanted to tell a deeper story than any previous Grand Theft Auto title in which they want players to form a stronger connection with protagonists Jason and Lucia like they did with Arthur Morgan . [ 55 ] Like with how players shaped their own Arthur Morgan in Red Dead Redemption 2 through dialogue choices with NPCs, Rockstar wanted to continue their stories being character-lead. [ 55 ] Jason and Lucia's safehouses across Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI operate like Red Dead Redemption 2 ' s interactable camps that provide an opportunity to build stronger character bonds. [ 55 ] In developing the relationship between Jason and Lucia and the role it plays in the narrative, writer Elizabeth Gauvey-Kern said the studio "wanted both protagonists to be vulnerable, tough, flawed, right sometimes, wrong sometimes, able to learn from each other". [ 56 ] Like with previous games, Grand Theft Auto VI satirises contemporary American culture, but Rockstar avoided making crude jokes about marginalised groups in the way prior games did. [ 29 ] [ 57 ] In creating a satirical version of America in the 2020s, Rupert Humphries said the world "has to be more over the top, more insane, more in your face" than the real world it is based on. [ 56 ] However, they wanted to be "careful" with what they satirise for fear that it could become dated fast given the length of the game's development period. Characters are designed to "feel somewhat timeless" in not using the most contemporary slang or referencing memes with a short shelf life. [ 58 ] A writing team based in Los Angeles was assembled, which included comedy writers, that was dedicated to NPC dialogue in the open world. [ 59 ] Open world writing director James Horton argued that there are "no background" characters in Grand Theft Auto VI depending on what the player chooses to pay attention to. [ 59 ] Technology Grand Theft Auto VI was built in the Rockstar Advanced Game Engine (RAGE), Rockstar's proprietary in-house engine , for ninth generation consoles . [ 60 ] It is Rockstar's first game specifically built for ninth generation consoles after porting Grand Theft Auto V to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X | S in 2022. Rockstar similarly used the 2014 ports of Grand Theft Auto V to PlayStation 4 and Xbox One as a way to become familiar with the console hardware before entering full production on Red Dead Redemption 2 . [ 61 ] Development heads Phil Hooker and Aaron Garbut claimed that the technology developed for Grand Theft Auto VI would lay the groundwork for future game development, reducing the development time between future Rockstar titles. [ 62 ] Due to the scale of the game's world, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson described the projections for the number of NPCs required to populate Leonida and the workload needed to realise it as "horrifying". [ 63 ] In order to help undertake such a workload, Rockstar Los Angeles was founded in 2023 as other Rockstar Games studios did not have the required production capabilities. [ 64 ] Rockstar Los Angeles were solely dedicated to NPC development with the goal of making NPCs more realistic in their appearance and behaviours. [ 65 ] NPCs are the most diverse of any Rockstar title, including physical variations in height and body size who can each have unique characteristics such as different types of freckles, moles, degrees of tanning, and tattoos. [ 66 ] [ 67 ] Nelson said that "we pushed interaction with NPCs as far as we could for Red Dead Redemption 2 " but Grand Theft Auto VI takes it "much further". [ 68 ] NPCs react more to player actions such as noticing the player eavesdropping on a conversation or nervously moving away upon seeing a masked Lucia drawing a gun before a store robbery. [ 68 ] Rockstar had begun working on NPC reactivity with Red Dead Redemption 2 and was able to get it working on previous generation consoles due to its more sparsely populated world as opposed to a denser, modern setting. [ 68 ] Rockstar avoided the use of procedural generation for creating NPCs in Grand Theft Auto VI , opting to handcraft NPCs who have their own personality traits determined by what district they are in, what they have seen, and how the player has behaved towards them. [ 69 ] The density of NPCs and simulating their associated AI and animation in the open world heavily taxes the Zen 2 CPU cores in ninth generation consoles. [ 70 ] Grand Theft Auto VI has over 600,000 unique NPC animations, up from 300,000 in Red Dead Redemption 2 and 55,000 in Grand Theft Auto V . [ 71 ] The increased variety of NPCs such as different heights created additional animation work. The animation set for a 5'6" NPC would not line up if it was to be directly mapped onto a taller 6'2" NPC. Rather than creating duplicate animation sets for NPCs of different heights, Rockstar developed a system of retargeting animations on the fly between NPCs of different proportions as a way to save on memory usage on consoles. [ 72 ] Grand Theft Auto VI ' s weather system was re-engineered from Red Dead Redemption 2 in order to achieve what Owen Shepherd, Rockstar vice president of art for lighting and rendering, called a "much more localised and persistent" world that "achieve[s] a sense of scale we haven't felt before". [ 73 ] Rockstar had previously implemented a volumetric clouds and fog system in Red Dead Redemption 2 while Grand Theft Auto VI brings more realistic cloud formations that dynamically move across the game world to emulate the variety of weather conditions in Florida and its tropical climate. [ 74 ] Like Florida, the state of Leonida in Grand Theft Auto VI can receive seasonal hurricanes and tropical storms with dynamic cloud formations allowing for localised rain showers in certain areas of the world. [ 75 ] During storms, visibility is reduced and winds become more prominent. After heavy rain spells, persistent puddles can form on the ground, rainwater can pour from rooftops and lamp posts and rainbows can be observed under the correct lighting conditions.
