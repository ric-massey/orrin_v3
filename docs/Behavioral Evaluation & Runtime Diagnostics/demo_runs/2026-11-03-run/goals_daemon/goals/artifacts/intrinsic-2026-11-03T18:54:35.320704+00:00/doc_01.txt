Infobox references Chemical compound Oxipurinol ( INN , or oxypurinol USAN ) is an inhibitor of xanthine oxidase . [ 1 ] It is an active metabolite of allopurinol and it is cleared renally. [ 2 ] In cases of renal disease, this metabolite will accumulate to toxic levels. By inhibiting xanthine oxidase, it reduces uric acid production. High serum uric acid levels may result in gout , kidney stones , and other medical conditions.
